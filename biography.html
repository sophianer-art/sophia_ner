Biography

Sophia Ner is an active cellist and Materials Engineering student at the University of British Columbia.

Sophia began playing the cello at age nine through her elementary school’s strings program. She continued playing cello in high school through the Byng Arts Mini Program and still plays through university. Sophia previously served as principal cellist of the Vancouver Youth Symphony Orchestra, where she was a concerto competition winner for the 2023/2024 season and performed Kabalevsky’s Second Cello Concerto in March 2024. Sophia played the fourth movement of Elgar’s Concerto with the Lord Byng Symphony Orchestra. Additionally, she was also named a finalist in the Vancouver Symphony Orchestra’s Future of Excellence Competition.

During her time at UBC, she plays in the cello section of the UBC Symphony Orchestra and has been a collaborative cellist with the UBC USingers Chorale Ensemble. She performed Tõnu Kõrvits’ Peegeldused tasasest maast (Reflections from a Plain), a piece for solo cello and choir. Sophia accompanied the choir to the American Choral Director’s Association (ACDA) conference in San Jose and the Podium Choral Conference in Victoria. For the summer of 2026, Sophia played in the National Youth Orchestra of Canada.

As an engineering student, Sophia is exploring how she can combine her interests in music and engineering. She enjoys using programming, simulations, and engineering analysis to investigate questions inspired by music and her experience as a cellist. Through her projects, she hopes to discover new ways that these two interests can intersect.

Outside of being and engineering student and cellist, Sophia enjoys biking around Vancouver and watching baseball. Some of her favourite composers are Rachmaninoff, Shostakovich, and Mahler.
