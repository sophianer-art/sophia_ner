Music Projects

Welcome to my music portfolio! Here, you can find information about my performances.

NYO Canada - June 2026 - July 2026

The National Youth Orchestra of Canada is a pre-professional training orchestra for young Canadian musicians. More than 500 musicians aged 16 to 28 audition every year, with the final orchestra being 90 members. The program is 6 weeks long, with the first 4 weeks filled with intensive training (chamber music, sectionals, tutti rehearsals) and concluding with a 2 week tour.

During the session, I played in three different chamber music ensembles. I played the second movement of the Elgar String Quartet, selection from Kaija Saariaho’s Neiges with the cello section, and Joaquín Turnia’s Scène andalouse.

The 2026 Canadiana Tour consisted of six concerts in Toronto, Stratford, Parry Sound, Kingston, Ottawa, and Saint Irénée. Highlights include performing Strauss’ Der Rosenkavalier and Prokofiev’s Fifth Symphony, and our Toronto concert being recorded by CBC. Later in the year, the tour album will be released.

I had so much fun playing in NYO Canada. As an engineering student, I have to find a way to balance playing cello with all of my coursework. Being able to immerse myself in musical study full time for six weeks was such a treat.

Collaboration with UBC USingers - January 2026 - May 2026

Dr. Graeme Langager reached out to me in January 2026 on the recommendation of the UBC Symphony Orchestra conductor to collaborate with the UBC USingers Chorale Ensemble for a couple of their upcoming performances. For this project, I performed Tõnu Kõrvits’ Peegeldused tasasest maast (Reflections from a Plain), a piece for solo cello and choir and accompanied the choir for Andrew Balfour’s Vision Chant. We performed these pieces for their February 2026 concert at the Chan Center and I accompanied the choir to the American Choral Director’s Association (ACDA) conference in San Jose in March 2026.

I was invited back to play the cello part in an arrangement of Radiohead’s Pyramid Song for their March concert at the Chan Center. Additionally, I accompanied the choir to the Podium Choral Conference in Victoria in May 2026, where we performed Pyramid Song and Vision Chant.

Mendelssohn Octet with Members of the VSO - July 2025

I was invited to be a part of the Mentoring Quartet for the 2025 Quiring Chamber Music Camp. This was a special project because we collaborated with four members of the Vancouver Symphony Orchestra to perform Mendelssohn’s Octet.

Aldo Parisot Cello Ensemble - September 2024 - June 2025

Super Shy - New Jeans, Arr. Sophia Ner

The Aldo Parisot Cello Ensemble was run by the Vancouver Academy of Music every week. We performed many genres of music, from pieces from the classical period to K-Pop songs.

I made cello arrangements of two K-Pop songs to be performed by this ensemble. In December 2024, we played my arrangement of Super Shy by NewJeans, and in June 2025, we played my arrangement of Supernova by aespa.

Elgar Cello Concerto with LBSO - June 2024

The Lord Byng Symphony Orchestra has a tradition of having graduating students audition to eventually perform a concerto with the orchestra.

I performed the fourth movement of Elgar’s Cello Concerto with LBSO in June 2024.

Kabalevsky Cello Concerto No. 2 with VYSO - March 2024

Every year, the Vancouver Youth Symphony Orchestra holds a concerto competition, where winners get to perform their concerto with the orchestra in one of their spring concerts.

In October 2023, I was named one of the winners and played the first and second movements of Kabalevsky’s Second Cello Concerto with the VYSO.

The concert took place in March 2024 at Christ Church Cathedral.
